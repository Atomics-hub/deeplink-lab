# Community code of conduct

DeepLink Lab is a technical community built around reproducible work. Participation requires respect for people and evidence.

Be direct about code and claims without being demeaning to contributors. Welcome questions, explain platform assumptions, credit prior work, and make room for people with different experience levels. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or use sexualized language or attention.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and communications that violate these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately, and focused on community safety.

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private security/contact channel. Maintainers will limit disclosure to the people needed to review the report and will avoid retaliation against good-faith reporters.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project events, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
